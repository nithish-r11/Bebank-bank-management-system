Account Freeze Policy & Fund Movement Rules

When an account is FROZEN, all outgoing debits (transfers, teller cash withdrawals, virtual card spending/issuance, fixed deposit creation, loan repayments) are strictly blocked to protect assets. Incoming credits (teller cash deposits, peer inbound transfers, fixed deposit maturity payouts, loan disbursements) remain permitted to maintain accounting integrity and allow settlement.
