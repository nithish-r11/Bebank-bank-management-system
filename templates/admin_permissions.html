Non-Negotiable Core Financial Boundaries

The following boundaries are hard-coded in the core banking architecture and cannot be bypassed or dynamically relaxed:

Final Loan Disbursement

Employees may only recommend approval or rejection. Only authorized Administrators can disburse funds into borrower accounts.

Debit Card Overrides

Employees see masked PANs only. Admin-level security overrides (permanent cancellation, limit resets) are reserved for Administrators.

Ticket Assignment

Employees can self-claim or release their own tickets. Arbitrary dispatch of support tickets across staff members requires Administrator role.

Audit Surveillance

Employees are strictly restricted to their own operational activity logs. System-wide audit visibility across all staff is restricted to Administrators.
